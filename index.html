FAQ

Common questions

Do I need fancy gear to use Varietal?

No. If you brew at home, Varietal helps. You can start simple and add detailed gear settings over time.

Can I track different beans and brew methods?

Yes. Track each bean and pair it with your preferred recipes and gear so you can compare results clearly.

How fast is onboarding?

Most users can set up their first brewer, bean, and grinder in a couple minutes with skip options built in.

What can I log for each brew?

You can log grind size, dose, water target, recipe ratio, tasting notes, and your final cup rating.
